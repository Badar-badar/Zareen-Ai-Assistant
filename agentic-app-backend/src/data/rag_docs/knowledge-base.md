# Store Knowledge Base & Policies

## 1. Shipping & Delivery Policy

### 1.1 Shipping Methods & Timeframes
- **Standard Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days. Free on all orders over $50; otherwise a flat rate of $4.99 applies.
- **Express Shipping**: 1 to 2 business days. Flat rate of $12.99.
- **Overnight Delivery**: Next business day delivery for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST. Flat rate of $24.99.
- **International Shipping**: 7 to 14 business days depending on destination country and customs clearance. Standard rate is $19.99 (customs duties/taxes are the responsibility of the recipient).

### 1.2 Order Processing Time
- All standard orders are processed within 24 to 48 hours (excluding weekends and public holidays).
- Once shipped, customers receive an email and SMS confirmation containing a carrier tracking link (USPS, FedEx, or DHL).

### 1.3 Changing Shipping Address
- Customers can change their shipping address within **2 hours** of placing an order by contacting support or updating it in their account dashboard under "Active Orders".
- Once an order has entered fulfillment or has been dispatched, the shipping address cannot be modified.

---

## 2. Return & Refund Policy

### 2.1 Return Window
- We offer a **30-day return policy** from the date of package delivery.
- Items must be in original, unused condition, with all tags attached and in original packaging.

### 2.2 Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned or refunded:
- Final clearance items (marked "Final Sale")
- Personal care, intimate apparel, and hygiene items
- Gift cards and downloadable software products
- Perishable goods or personalized/custom-engraved merchandise

### 2.3 Refund Process & Timelines
- Once your return package is received at our fulfillment warehouse, inspection takes **2 to 3 business days**.
- Approved refunds will be automatically credited to the original method of payment (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay).
- Depending on your financial institution, funds typically reflect in your account within **5 to 10 business days**.
- Shipping fees from the original order are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.

### 2.4 Return Shipping Fee
- Returns for store credit are **100% free** (prepaid return label provided).
- Returns for refund to original payment method incur a **$5.99 return postage fee**, which will be deducted from the total refund amount.

---

## 3. Exchanges & Damaged Items

### 3.1 Size & Color Exchanges
- We offer free exchanges for different sizes or colors of the same product within 30 days of receipt, subject to stock availability.
- Once the exchange request is submitted, a prepaid return shipping label will be generated immediately.

### 3.2 Defective or Damaged Items
- If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please notify customer support within **48 hours of delivery**.
- Please provide your Order Number, a brief description of the issue, and clear photos of the damaged item and packaging.
- We will issue a free instant replacement or a 100% full refund including shipping fees.

---

## 4. Order Cancellation Policy

### 4.1 Cancellation Window
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge within **1 hour** of placement by clicking "Cancel Order" on the order confirmation page or contacting support.
- If the order has already been processed or dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible; you will need to initiate a return after delivery.

### 5.1 Accepted Payment Options

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: How do I track my order status?
**Answer:** You can track your order in real-time by entering your Order ID and email on our "Track Order" page, or by clicking the tracking URL provided in your shipment confirmation email.

### Q2: Can I apply multiple coupon codes to a single order?
**Answer:** Only one promotional discount code can be applied per order. Discount codes cannot be stacked with automatic sitewide sales unless explicitly stated.

### Q3: What happens if my package is marked delivered but I haven't received it?
**Answer:** Please check around your porch, mailbox, and with neighbors. If you still cannot locate it after 24 hours, contact customer support. We will launch an investigation with the carrier and issue a replacement or refund if deemed lost in transit.

### Q4: Do you offer international warranty on products?
**Answer:** All hardware, electronics, and watch products come with a 1-year manufacturer limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
